The story

Made in Ashburton.
Loud on purpose.

Seoul Fire started with one obsession: Korean fried chicken done properly. Brined overnight, hand-coated, twice-fried, and tossed in glazes we make ourselves.

You'll find us on East Street, feeding the Ashburton community the same way — whether you're dining in with us, grabbing takeaway, or ordering to the couch.
