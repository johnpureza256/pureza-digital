Our story

Small-town salon.
Big-city finish.

Styles & Smiles is a one-chair salon on Tancred Street — which is exactly how we like it. No hand-offs, no waiting around, no explaining your usual to someone new every visit.

The same stylist meets you at the door, does your consult, your cut and your colour, and remembers how you had it last time. That’s the whole model.
